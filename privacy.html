Last updated: September 9, 2026

SiteSure Cleaning Services respects the privacy of visitors and prospective clients. This policy explains the types of information we may collect through this website and how that information may be used.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, evaluate service requests, prepare proposals, schedule or confirm walkthroughs, communicate about requested services, and operate our business.

We may use website hosting, form-processing, and email providers to operate the website and deliver inquiries. These providers may process information on our behalf subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or work with service providers that support our operations.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry information only as reasonably necessary for business, legal, accounting, customer-service, or recordkeeping purposes.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information submitted through the website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of information you previously submitted, subject to legal and recordkeeping obligations.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to latasha@sitesurecleaning.com.
