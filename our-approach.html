We Noticed It Before You Did

Observation Is Part of Good Service.

During routine cleaning, SiteSure may observe visible concerns that affect presentation, usability, or day-to-day facility readiness. Those observations are communicated factually so the appropriate facility contact can decide what happens next.

We do not diagnose conditions, perform repairs unless separately scoped, investigate hazards, or certify building, environmental, pest, mold, or code conditions.

Examples of visible observations

Water leaks or standing water
Damaged or leaking fixtures
Broken or empty dispensers
Flickering or burned-out lights
Unusual odors
Possible pest evidence
Damaged flooring concerns
Door, lock, or hardware concerns
Low facility supplies
Other visible concerns
